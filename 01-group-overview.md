## What IMA AI is

IMA AI (imaai.my) — a Malaysia-based AI-native agency (GitHub org `imaai-my`) building websites, brand assets, chat assistants and short-form drama content for itself and client "ventures." meilen is the CEO and sole ratifying authority for standards, brand, and governance decisions.

## Repo map

**Company-internal:**
- `imaai-standards` — the company rulebook, 8 domains × 5 tiers, cited by ID (`IMA-<DOM>-<TIER>-<nn>`). Read-write from this hub project only.
- `imaai-assets` — brand Art Bible / Asset Card system (`IMA-BRD-STD-01`).
- `imaai-website` — the company's own site, imaai.my, hub-owned (`IMA-AIO-STD-02`).
- `meilen-reference` — local credentials store (`.env`), not a git repo — the personal-account analog of `credentials.env`.
- `meilen-hub` (this repo) — the hub / records repo.

**Client / product:**
- `bzubzu-data` — raw Shopee performance data for client BZUBZU (MY + SG), staff-shareable, no commercial terms.
- `laundryhub-00` — client KB for LaundryHub Asia (client: Infinite Verkz International Sdn Bhd), paired with `laundryhub-website`.

**Adjacent, outside `imaai-my`:**
- `bzubzu-data-screencap` (OneDrive folder, not git) — a separate, narrowly-scoped Cowork project that only captures Shopee LIVE Insight screenshots; explicitly forbidden from touching any spreadsheet. The daily BZUBZU live-report workbook (`captured-master.xlsx`) is generated by a separate process/session this hub does not currently have visibility into.

## Confidentiality

Commercial terms (rates, quotations, contract values) live ONLY in this repo's `06-xxx` client records, never in `imaai-standards`, `imaai-website`, or any client/product repo. Secrets live ONLY in `meilen-reference/.env` (personal) or `imaai-reference/credentials.env` (org), never committed anywhere.
